Search engine optimization (SEO) is the work of making your website easy for Google and other search engines to find, understand and recommend. What We Do to Improve Your SEO

Every site is different, so the work follows what your audit finds. These are the improvements that most often make the difference for a small business.

Keyword Research

We find the words and phrases your customers actually type into Google, then make sure your pages answer them.

On-Page Optimization

Page titles, descriptions, headings and content rewritten so search engines and people both understand what you offer.

Local SEO

Your Google Business Profile, service area and business details tuned so nearby customers find you first.

Speed & Mobile Fixes

Slow or hard-to-use pages get pushed down in search results. We fix what is holding your site back on phones and desktops.

Structured Data

Behind-the-scenes markup that tells search engines exactly who you are, what you do and where you do it.

Technical Cleanup

Sitemaps, broken links, duplicate pages and indexing problems resolved so every page that should appear in search can.

Before-and-After Google Search Console Analysis

Google Search Console is Google's own record of how your website performs in search. We use it to show you where you started, what we recommend changing, and what actually improved.

Before

A baseline from your own Google data

Before changing anything, we connect to Google Search Console and record exactly where your site stands today.

  • Clicks and impressions from Google Search
  • Average position and click-through rate
  • The searches and pages that bring visitors in
  • Indexing, mobile and page experience issues
SEO Audit

What can be updated, in plain language

We review your site page by page and give you a prioritized list of what to update and why it matters.

  • Page titles, descriptions and headings
  • Content gaps and missed keywords
  • Speed, mobile and technical problems
  • Local listings and business details
After

The same numbers, measured again

Once the improvements are live, we run the same Search Console analysis so you can see what changed.

  • Side-by-side before-and-after comparison
  • New searches your site now appears for
  • Movement in position, clicks and impressions
  • Recommended next steps to keep growing

SEO for AI-Enabled Search

Search is changing. More people now get their answer from an AI summary at the top of Google, or ask an assistant like ChatGPT or Gemini to recommend a local business. Those tools draw on websites they can clearly read and trust. The same foundations that help Google understand your site help them too, and we build on those with three things AI search rewards.

Clear, Direct Answers

AI tools quote websites that answer questions plainly. We write and structure your content, including FAQs, so your business is the easy answer to pull from.

Data AI Can Read

Structured data spells out your services, location and hours in a format AI search tools rely on to describe your business accurately.

Consistent Everywhere

AI cross-checks what it finds. We keep your business details matching across your website, Google Business Profile and other listings so you are a source it can trust.
