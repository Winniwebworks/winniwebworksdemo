About The Company

Professional websites made accessible—for any business, any vision

Located in Lakes Region, New Hampshire, Winni WebWorks is a small business dedicated to helping other local businesses grow their online presence. We understand that many companies either don’t have a website yet or have one that no longer meets their needs—and that’s where we come in. Our goal is to make professional websites accessible to everyone, no matter the budget or size of the vision. Whether you’re looking for a simple landing page, a full e-commerce store, or something completely custom, we’ll work with you to bring it to life. We believe every business deserves a website that reflects who they are and helps them reach more people in their community and beyond.

About The Developer


Where data-driven thinking meets purposeful web design

Sara Huntley

Owner and Developer

With over a decade of experience in data analytics, business intelligence, and technology solutions, Sara brings a strong foundation in both technical development and business strategy to her work. Her background includes designing data systems, building reporting solutions, and developing tools that help organizations make smarter, data-driven decisions.
Holding both a Bachelor’s degree in Business Data Analytics and a Master’s degree in Business Analytics, Sara combines formal education with hands-on experience across complex, large-scale projects. She has led development efforts, built data pipelines, and worked closely with stakeholders to deliver reliable, efficient solutions.
Today, she applies that same analytical and problem-solving mindset to web development—creating websites that are not only visually engaging, but also functional, secure, and built to support long-term growth.
